El Pollo Loco

Choose your difficulty level

Gameplay

Try to kill chickens by jumping on them or hitting them with bottles.
The final boss can only be damaged by bottles, so keep an eye on your bottle count.
Collect bottles to fill your count.
Collect 10 coins to replenish your life.
Try to complete the level within the specified time.
Be careful, the boss spawns chickens.

Controls

Move left: A or Arrow left

Move rigt: W or Arrow right

Jump: Spacebar

Throw Items: T
